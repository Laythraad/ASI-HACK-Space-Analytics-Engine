--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Satellite Module must serve as a high-precision Earth Observation & Orbit Command Dashboard:

1. Satellite Fleet Grid & Selector:
   - Dynamic Selector: Switch between Earth Observation Satellites (e.g., Sentinel, Landsat, MODIS) and Custom Mission Satellites.
   - Status Indicators: Operational (Green), Degraded Sensor (Yellow), Signal Gap (Red), Calibration Mode (Blue).

2. Real-Time Telemetry & Spatial Metrics Panel:
   - Metrics Display: NORAD ID, Mission Type, Altitude (km), Orbital Velocity (km/s), Inclination (deg), Spatial Resolution (m/pixel), Revisit Period (Days), Sensor Temperature (deg C).

3. Interactive Geospatial GIS Coverage Map:
   - Interactive 3D/2D Globe displaying live orbital footprints, swath paths, satellite trajectories, and coverage gaps.
   - Layer Controls: Toggle Nighttime Lights, Optical RGB, Thermal Surface Bands, Vegetation Index (NDVI), Cloud Coverage overlays.

4. Sensor Band Radiance & Signal Inspector:
   - Multi-Spectral Band Spectrum Visualizer (Plotting Band Wavelength vs. Radiance Flux).
   - Sensor Noise Diagnostic (Signal-to-Noise Ratio SNR calculation over time).

5. Satellite Detailed Inspection Modal / Page:
   - Granular breakdown of individual satellite passes, raw telemetry logs, acquired image frames, and anomaly flags.
